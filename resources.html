Quality Checklist

Keep these points in mind when completing moderation work.

Be Thorough

Review the information available to you before making a classification or submitting a report.

Be Consistent

Apply the task instructions consistently across similar cases and avoid assumptions that are not supported by the content.

Be Clear

Use concise, factual explanations when a task asks you to document your decision or findings.

Safety & Escalation

When a situation requires additional review, document the relevant facts and follow the instructions attached to your task.

Document the Incident

Capture the relevant details without adding assumptions or unnecessary personal information.

Protect Sensitive Information

Handle personal and sensitive information according to the applicable task instructions and security requirements.

Follow Escalation Steps

If the task provides an escalation process, use the specified channel and include the required information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Where can I see available tasks?

Open the Tasks page to review the available task categories, requirements, instructions, and payment information.

How do I know what a task requires?

Open the task details before applying. The instructions and requirements shown there should be reviewed carefully before you begin.

Where can I check withdrawal requirements?

Visit the Withdrawals page for the available payment methods, minimum withdrawal amounts, and processing information.

What should I do if I encounter a security-related incident?

Follow the escalation instructions provided for your task and document the relevant facts clearly and accurately.
