Verify Your Identity

Before you can access your moderator dashboard, we need to confirm your identity.

Step 1: Identity Verification

Select one of the accepted identity documents and upload it.

Step 2: Upload a selfie

Upload a selfie for the identity check.

Step 3: Address Verification

Select an accepted address document and upload a document showing your current residential address.

Step 4: Verify your phone number

Select your region and enter a valid phone number from the United States, Europe, or Asia.

Account Verification Timeline

1. Documents submitted Your identity document, selfie, address document, and phone verification are submitted for review.
2. Document review — 2–3 business days The submitted documents will be reviewed within 2–3 business days.
3. Verification result You will be notified once the review is complete.
